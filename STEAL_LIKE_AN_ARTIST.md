# Steal Like an Artist: how Lanternfall borrows without copying

This project follows the method in Austin Kleon's book *Steal Like an Artist*, paraphrased here. Every creator is built from influences. The honest move is to study many of them, keep the principle, discard the surface, and add something of your own. Copying one source is theft. Remixing many sources through your own taste is how new work starts.

## The rule we use: borrow the idea, never the expression

| Fine to borrow (ideas, mechanics, conventions) | Never borrow (expression) |
|---|---|
| Side-scrolling action with a sword and a spell | Another game's code, art, maps, music, sounds |
| Touch layout with move on the left and actions on the right | Another game's characters, names, dialogue, story |
| Checkpoints that save the game | Another game's exact level layouts |
| Ability-gated secrets | Another game's title, logo or icon style |
| Enemies that telegraph, then commit, then recover | Anything that makes players think this is that game |

## Influence ledger

| Influence (kind, not a copy) | What we studied | What Lanternfall does with it |
|---|---|---|
| Touch-first side-scrolling action RPGs (the genre) | Three-zone thumb layout, short play segments | Own layout and labels. Light, not mana, is the resource. |
| Ability-gated exploration games | A new power opens routes that were closed | One power, Flare, makes hidden Lumen platforms solid. The secret ledge in the hollow uses the same rule. |
| Precision platformers | Coyote time and jump buffering (widely documented techniques) | Tuned in our own constants, tested in `tests/test_runner.gd`. |
| Boss-fight design in action games | Telegraph, commit, punish window | Gloomback Crawler: shudder and flashing eyes, lunge, then a harmless recovery. |
| Moonlit pine forests and fireflies (real world) | Silhouette layers, cold palette, one warm accent | Teal and amber palette. Warm amber always means "you can interact with this". |

Kleon also suggests stealing from outside your own field. Next-step idea for this project: draw regions and creatures from Philippine folklore and landscapes you know. Research it respectfully, and favor public folklore over any single living culture's sacred material.

- The light mechanic: Flare plus Lumen platforms

- Enemy design and the level layout of Lanternroot Hollow

## Pre-release originality checklist

1. Search the app stores and your national trademark office for "Lanternfall". Change the name if anything is close.
2. Never use the words "Swordigo" or "sequel" in the title, description, tags, screenshots or store keywords.
3. Keep `ASSET_LICENSES.md` accurate. If you add any third-party asset, record its license before committing it.
4. Replace every placeholder with your own art or properly licensed art.
5. Have a person who knows IP law review the store listing before a paid release. This file is guidance, not legal advice.

## About the provisional title in the skill file

The skill suggests "Swordigo 2: The Legacy of the Lost Kingdom". Do not ship that. "Swordigo" belongs to someone else, and "2" claims to be their sequel. That is the fastest route to a takedown. The working title here is **Lanternfall: Echoes of the Sunken Crown**.
