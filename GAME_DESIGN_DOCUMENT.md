# Lanternfall: Echoes of the Sunken Crown. Game Design Document

Version 0.1 (vertical slice). Status of each item is in `README.md`.

## 1. Concept and pillars

You are the Wickbearer, carrier of a lantern-blade, walking through a land where the old guardian lights have gone out. Light is both weapon and key.

1. **Light is the verb.** Flare fights and opens routes. Lumen platforms exist only while lit.
2. **Fair fights.** Every dangerous move is telegraphed and followed by a punish window.
3. **Thumb-first.** Short stretches between lantern posts, big touch buttons, no tiny precision input.
4. **Original identity.** See `STEAL_LIKE_AN_ARTIST.md`.

## 2. Platform and audience

Android phones in landscape, plus desktop for development. Built for low-end hardware: Compatibility renderer, no textures, flat vector shapes, small audio. Audience: teens and adults who like action platformers.

## 3. Story and characters

Ancient guardian lights once held the dark back. The last one failed and the Hollowing began. The Wickbearer finds the Wickblade, a sword whose pommel is a lantern, and follows the guardians' trail toward the Sunken Crown.

- **Wickbearer:** hooded traveler, teal tunic, amber scarf. Name to be chosen.
- **Gloombacks:** shelled crawlers bred by the dark, purple shells with pale spore patches and amber eyes.
- Planned: a lantern keeper NPC at posts, region guardians as bosses.

## 4. Core loop

Explore, fight or avoid, collect Glimmer Shards, light posts to save, use Flare to reveal routes and secrets, reach the objective.

## 5. Movement and combat (implemented values)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Run speed | 270 px/s, accel 2000, decel 2600, air accel 1500 |
| Jump | velocity 560, gravity 1600 (x1.25 falling), tap cut 0.45, rise about 98 px |
| Coyote time / jump buffer | 0.10 s / 0.12 s |
| Sword | 0.30 s swing, hit window 0.07 to 0.20 s, 1 damage, one hit per target per swing, attack allowed in air |
| Hurt | 1 damage, 1.1 s invulnerability, 0.25 s stun, knockback |
| Health / light | 5 hearts / 100 light |

## 6. Magic and progression

- **Flare** (done): costs 25 light, regenerates 9/s, travels 560 px/s for 1.5 s, deals 2 damage, reveals Lumen platforms within 130 px for 7 s (they flicker for the last 1.2 s).
- Planned: Frost, Storm and Arcane variants with their own costs, effects and upgrades.
- **Progression** (planned): shards buy health, light and attack upgrades at posts. Only the shard counter exists today.

## 7. Enemies and bosses

- **Gloomback Crawler** (done). Patrol, notice within 280 px, chase, 0.55 s windup (squash, shudder, flashing eyes), 0.28 s lunge, 0.65 s harmless recovery. 3 hit points. Drops a shard.
- Planned: Cinder Wisp (flying), Ashplate Guard (armored), elite variants.
- Planned boss: the Rootmother, with root-slam telegraphs and two phases.

## 8. World

1. **Emerald Wilds** (slice: Lanternroot Hollow, about 4000 px wide, 2 posts, 5 enemies, 21 shards, 1 secret)
2. Crystal Caverns
3. Kingdom of Ash
4. Frostbound Mountains
5. Ancient Sky Temple

Regions beyond the first are design targets only.

## 9. UI and controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | A/D or arrows | D-pad or left stick | left and right buttons, bottom left |
| Jump | Space, W, Up | A | JUMP, bottom right |
| Slash | J or X | X | SLASH |
| Flare | K or C | B | FLARE |
| Pause | Esc or P | Start | II, top right |

Menu: Continue, New Game, Settings (music, effects, touch controls Auto/Always/Never), Credits, Exit. HUD: hearts, light bar with a marker at one Flare cost, shard count, hint toasts.

## 10. Art and audio direction

Cold teal world, warm amber for anything interactive, cyan for light-made things, purple for enemies. Parallax layers: moon, far peaks, two pine layers, drifting fog, fireflies. Current art is **temporary placeholder geometry** drawn in code. Audio is generated by `tools/generate_audio.py`: 16 effects and one ambient loop, also temporary.

## 11. Technical architecture

- Godot 4.4, GDScript, GL Compatibility renderer.
- Autoloads: `GameState` (run state, input map, settings), `SaveSystem` (atomic JSON save), `Sfx` (sound pool, music).
- Scenes are thin shells. Nodes and shapes are built in code, which keeps the project text-only and diff-friendly. Move to hand-authored scenes and sprite sheets when real art arrives.
- The level layout is data: rectangles, positions and ids at the top of `scripts/levels/level.gd`.
- Collision layers are named in `scripts/managers/layers.gd`.

## 12. Milestones and risks

| Milestone | Scope |
|---|---|
| M1 (done) | Vertical slice, tests, docs |
| M2 | Real art and animation, boss, device tuning |
| M3 | Second region, shop and upgrades, second spell |

Risks: Android build untested; touch feel unproven on a phone; placeholder art may hide readability problems; scope creep before the core loop is fun.
